Labels

1 exact_dup — Merge

Same durable rule / same instruction. Safe to collapse into one knowledge node. Content may be paraphrased but decision is identical.

2 related — Link only

Same topic family or project context. Constellation edge is useful, but do not merge (different rules, stages, or scopes).

3 unrelated — No edge

Path-only / template-only similarity, protocol junk, or different problems. Should not link and must not merge.

Decision checklist

  • Would merging lose a distinct rule? → related or unrelated
  • Only share path/prefix (e.g. same HPC dir)? → usually unrelated
  • Same fix, same trigger, same action? → exact_dup
  • High emb + low text is often a trap (hard negative)
  • When unsure, prefer related over exact_dup
